## 3. Benchmarks and day rates

- **Whito price file (June 2026)**, whito.co.uk/research/website-design-costs-uk/ [S]: **published fixed packages median £520, middle half £399–£898 (n=28)**. Separately, 27 firms showing a one-off "from" price: middle figure £600, range £100–£12,000; 13 of 27 start below £500. 5 firms were monthly-only, £29–£795/mo. Built-to-brief ranges: freelancer 5-page £1,500–£3,000, agency £3,000–£5,000, e-commerce £5,000–£20,000+. Where VAT was stated, prices were ex VAT. In Birmingham, only 1 of 9 page-one web designers showed a price. Whito is a vendor, but it publishes its method and a CC BY CSV.
- **Bark, Birmingham** [S]: small-business site (5–10 pages) £200–£1,000; bespoke £1,000–£3,250; e-commerce £625–£6,250+. Marketplace guide, not a survey.
- **No independent survey (FSB, ONS or government) of SME website spend was found.** Every other "2026 cost guide" was a vendor blog.
- **Day rates.** ITJobsWatch, Web Development contracts, 6 months to 19 Nov 2026 (itjobswatch.co.uk/contracts/uk/web%20development.do) [S]: **median £525/day** (25th–75th percentile £438–£600; UK excluding London median £475; 98 rates quoted). These are contract (IR35-world) rates, not small-business project rates. Shopify freelancers: £300–£600/day (Design Super [S]). Freelance hourly: £20–£75 (freelance-webdesign.co.uk [S]).

## 4. VAT

- Threshold **£90,000** taxable turnover over a rolling 12 months; voluntary registration is allowed below it (gov.uk/register-for-vat [S]).
- **Advertising rule** (ASA advice "Compulsory costs and charges: VAT"; rulings Interparcel A13-233682 and Valley Services A13-218472 [s]): prices addressed to consumers must include VAT. A "+VAT" price is allowed only if *everyone* addressed can recover VAT, and then the VAT must be stated prominently. Sponsored ads aimed at a mix of business and non-business buyers were held to need inc-VAT prices. A Facebook/WhatsApp ad aimed at small businesses will reach many sole traders who are not VAT-registered, so **quote inc VAT, or the full price if you don't charge VAT**.
- **How small, unregistered sellers quote:** the price is the whole price. Webwinton: "Currently no VAT, though that may change"; HELYI: "No VAT to add" [S]. Whito: most freelancers under the threshold don't charge VAT, and agencies almost always do. *Check whether EAZY ACCESS LTD is VAT-registered. CLAUDE.md gives no VAT number.* If it isn't, "£1,250, no VAT to add" competes with "£1,500 +VAT" (£1,800) from VAT-registered studios. If it registers later, keep the ad price inc VAT (or absorb the VAT).

## 6. Starting-price options [E, my recommendation, the owner decides]

Assumes no VAT is charged. If the company is VAT-registered, these are the inc-VAT figures to advertise.

**"A small business website"**

| Option | Ad price | Position | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **from £795** | Top of the published-package band, about the sample median | Easy yes from a cold Facebook audience; still about 4.5× cheaper than two years at £149/mo. Undersells a hand-built, Lighthouse-100 site; at about 3 days of work it's roughly £265/day, below the bottom of the contract range. Puts you next to £399–£899 template shops. |
| B | **from £1,250** (recommended) | Entry to the small-studio band; next to Brambla £1,200, under Swiftcase's £1,800 inc | Prices the quality and ownership. The ad's argument is the two-year sum: "£1,250 once, yours, vs £3,576 over two years at £149/mo, and theirs". Loses buyers looking only at the monthly number. |
| C | **from £1,950** | Middle of built-to-brief freelancer | Clear premium signal and fewer, better-fit enquiries. Weak for a Facebook/WhatsApp audience with no case studies from paying clients yet. |

**"An online shop"**

| Option | Ad price | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| A | **from £1,950** | At the freelancer median; competitive with JB and Webwinton. Thin margin once payments, delivery, VAT settings and product loading are counted. |
| B | **from £2,750** (recommended) | Bottom of the small-studio band, with Design Super's £2,500 and we-dev's £2,650+VAT (£3,180 inc). Leaves room to load products and train. |
| C | **from £3,500** | Matches Sayu and Brambla. Hard to justify with no client shops in the portfolio yet. |

**Stages:** price each stage so it stands alone and the client can stop after any of them.
1. **Plan and design**: scope, page list, the home page plus one inner page designed, launch date fixed. About 25–30% of the total: **£350** on a £1,250 site, £750 on a £2,750 shop. About 0.7 of a contract day-rate median [S: £525/day]. Gives a low-risk first step for the ad.
2. **Build**: remaining pages, contact form, speed and accessibility checks. About 50%: **£650** / £1,400.
3. **Launch and handover**: domain and hosting in the client's name, logins handed over, training. About 20–25%: **£250** / £600.
Extra page: £100–£150 (market £75–£150 [S: Webwinton, JB, Blue Whale]).
