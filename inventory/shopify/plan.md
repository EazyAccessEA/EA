## Agreement clause (Partner Program Agreement s.2.4 fee disclosure; s.6 requires a written agreement with the client)

> We build your shop in a Shopify development store, which we hold while we work on it. Before launch we transfer it to you. From then on the store, its Shopify plan and every login are yours. While you stay on a paid Shopify plan, Shopify pays us a referral commission of up to 20% of your monthly subscription. Shopify sets that percentage. Your Shopify price and our fees are the same either way.
