What it does

You pick a size and a cloth, see the price for that exact size, and pay through Stripe.

Most of the sizes are for girls, so the size picker does most of the work, and the price changes with the size you choose.

Smaller calls

Prices are checked on the server

Someone once bought an adult jilbab at the children's price by sending the cheaper price with an adult length. Each price now lists the exact sizes it covers, and the server refuses any pairing that doesn't match.

Postage too

The basket can't set its own delivery charge. The server counts the garments and works out the charge by weight, and free delivery kicks in above a set spend.

The confirmation email is the contract

UK consumer law says a shop has to confirm an order in a form you can keep. After payment the shop emails an acceptance, with the cancellation terms copied word for word from its returns page.

Each payment handled once

When Stripe reports a payment, the shop keeps a tiny marker against that event so it is never handled twice. No customer details are kept.
