Why Workers

What changes when the site runs on Cloudflare instead of a usual web host.

Cloudflare bills you directly for anything past its free allowance. We tell you which plan fits before you decide.

A usual web host

  • A server or hosting plan to keep updated
  • Pages sent from one place, wherever the visitor is
  • Forms and email added through plugins

On Cloudflare Workers

  • Cloudflare runs the servers and keeps them updated
  • Pages sent from Cloudflare's network, close to each visitor
  • Forms, spam checks and email written into the site

What we build

Pages, forms, and anything the site needs to store, such as orders or bookings.

This site is one. Its contact form checks for spam with Cloudflare Turnstile, then emails the enquiry to us. Sagheerah, our own shop, takes card payments through Stripe on the same setup.
