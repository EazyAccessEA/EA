# Why our sites look the same (measured 16 Nov 2026)

Computed styles from the six live homepages at 1440×900. Raw data: `sameness-audit.json`.

| Trait | Count | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Light ground | 6/6 | 4 warm off-whites (#FDFCF8, #F7F7F4, #EFE8DA, #F2EBDA) and 2 pure whites |
| Heavy, tightly tracked sans headline | 5/6 | Inter Tight 800, Geist 700, Inter Display 900, Bricolage 800, Clash 600. Letter-spacing −0.02em to −0.04em |
| Small uppercase labels | 5/6 | PagePerfect 57, Sagheerah 38, FarmCompanion 28, Flintmere 19. All mono on PagePerfect and Flintmere |
| Full-width hairline rules | 6/6 | Up to 25 on one page (PagePerfect) |
| Square corners on buttons | 4/6 | — |
| One saturated accent on a neutral ground | 6/6 | Red, yellow, amber, red, red, near-black |

All six sites (and both directions drawn yesterday) share one formula: a neutral light page, a big heavy sans headline, mono uppercase labels, hairlines and one hot accent. It is the 2024–26 "tasteful indie" template. The studio's own brand has to break at least three of those five traits on purpose, or it will look like a seventh copy.
