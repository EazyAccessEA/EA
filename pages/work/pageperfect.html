PagePerfect

A journal of essays on typography and book layout. Every claim that could be argued has a note naming its sources and grading the evidence.

Who for
Typographers, and authors who set their own books
Built with
Next.js, Cloudflare Workers, Typst
Live at
pageperfect.studio

What it does

It tests design rules against the evidence, and says what reading research has and hasn't found.

Each disputable claim carries a note that names its sources and grades the evidence from A to E. The journal is also a paperback.

Smaller calls

Check every claim, then rewrite

Every claim was traced to its source and the essays rewritten to say only what the sources support. Essays that existed mainly to sell were taken down.

A missing font stops the build

A missing font once made the book's spine far wider than the pages inside it. Now the build fails instead of quietly swapping in another font.

Checked, not typed

An early note said the PDFs had no accessibility errors before anyone had run a validator. When one was run, every edition failed. Claims about the PDFs are now read from the files themselves.
