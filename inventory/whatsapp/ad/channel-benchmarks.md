# Channel benchmarks: Meta click-to-WhatsApp vs Google Search, £30/month

Researched 19 Nov 2026. Read-only web research; no ad accounts opened. All figures are third-party benchmarks or platform documentation, not this studio's results.

Flags: **S** = sourced figure as published. **S-US** = sourced, US data, used as a proxy. **E** = my estimate or derivation (method stated). **U** = published but unverifiable (no method, or the author has a commercial interest).

FX used for USD → GBP conversions: $1 = £0.76 (E, rough; not checked against a dated rate).

## 3. What could not be verified

- No UK, or any independent, benchmark for **cost per messaging conversation started** on click-to-WhatsApp ads. Every published figure is vendor-sourced with no sample.
- No published **reach-to-impressions (frequency)** figure for small-budget local campaigns. Frequency inputs are guesses.
- No **Birmingham CPC** for any web design keyword; GBP figures are converted from a tool that reports USD and doesn't disclose its method.
- **Search volumes** for Birmingham terms come only from one self-interested site that contradicts itself (590 vs 1,200 for the same phrase). Keyword Planner in a Google Ads account is the only reliable check.
- **Meta's minimum daily budget in GBP** for the Messages objective: Meta no longer publishes a figure; Ads Manager shows it at setup.
- **WordStream 2025 Facebook Business Services** figures came from a search summary; the page returned 403.
- **Google Smart Bidding minimum conversion data** not checked this session.
- Superads CPM currency is inferred (£), not printed.
