Cookies

Last updated: 16 November 2026.

This site doesn't set any cookies of its own. There's no analytics, advertising or tracking, which is why you didn't see a cookie banner.

What other services may store

  • The spam check on the contact form, Cloudflare Turnstile, may store a little data in your browser to tell people from bots. It's needed to keep the form secure, so the law doesn't require us to ask first.
  • The fonts come from Adobe Fonts. Adobe says the service doesn't set cookies, and our own check found none.

If this changes

We'll update this page before anything new is added, and we'll ask you before setting anything that isn't essential.

Questions: info@eazyaccess.org. How we handle the information you send us is in our privacy notice.
