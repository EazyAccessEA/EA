Who we are

We decide how your information is used, which makes us the data controller. For anything about your information, email info@eazyaccess.org.

What we collect

  • From the contact form: your name, email address, what you need and your message.
  • If you email us: whatever your email contains.
  • When you visit: your IP address and browser details reach our hosting provider, Cloudflare, as they would with any website. We don't run analytics or advertising.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry and, if you'd like to go ahead, to agree the work with you. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before a contract (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).

We also use your IP address and the spam check on the form to stop automated abuse. That's our legitimate interest in keeping the form working (Article 6(1)(f)).

We never sell your information, and we don't use it for marketing.

Who handles it for us

  • Cloudflare hosts this site and runs the spam check on the form.
  • Resend delivers your message to our inbox and sends you a confirmation email.
  • Google Workspace runs our email, which is where your message is kept.
  • Adobe serves the fonts on this site, so it receives your IP address.

Some of these companies are based in the United States, so your information may be processed there. Each one works under a data processing agreement with the safeguards UK law requires for transfers abroad.

How long we keep it

This website doesn't store your message. It passes it straight to our inbox. We keep enquiry emails for two years after we last heard from you, then delete them. If your enquiry turns into a project, we keep what's needed for that project and our accounts.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, object to how we use it, or ask us to limit its use. Email info@eazyaccess.org and we'll reply within one month, as the law requires.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
