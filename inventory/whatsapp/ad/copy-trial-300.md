# £300 trial: three ads (Meta click-to-WhatsApp)

Price ruling (owner, 21 Nov 2026): **£1,250 + VAT**. The company isn't VAT-registered yet, so these ads can't run until the VAT application is in and the effective date of registration has started (gov.uk: "You cannot include VAT on your invoices until you get your VAT registration number, but you can increase your prices to account for the VAT"). Each ad shows the total as well, because CAP 3.18 expects VAT-inclusive prices unless the audience is clearly business-only.

Setup for all three: one ad set, Birmingham + 25 miles, ages 25–65+, objective Engagement → Messaging conversations (WhatsApp), £10 a day for 30 days, Advantage+ placements off (Facebook and Instagram feeds only). Meta splits spend between the three versions; don't edit for the first 7 days.

Every claim below maps to a promise in CLAUDE.md (reply in two working days; one person; client owns domain, hosting and logins; fixed price per stage, nothing extra unless agreed in writing; launch date agreed first) or to the owner's price ruling.
