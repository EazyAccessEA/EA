# Deep visual research (16 Nov 2026)

35 Awwwards sites (SOTD and nominees, 2026) were rendered in Playwright: desktop at 0%, 40% and 80% scroll, plus phone. 24 were reviewed by eye.
- Contact sheet: research-shots/_sheet.png
- Per-site shots: research-shots/<slug>-{d0,d40,d80,m0}.png

## Five ownable directions
1. **Flat colour world plus a custom wordmark** (Warm & Fuzzy, K95, RPA). Recognisable as a thumbnail and cheap to build.
2. **Mascot or illustration world** (House of Giants, at 0.8MB with no canvas: prices and "Request a free fit call" are in the first mobile view). Extrafazant and Papercraft are close.
3. **Name as layout** (Two Halves: "You build it physically / We design it digitally").
4. **Text-art texture** (AINO, 2XA). Fights screen readers and readability.
5. **Gated immersive** (KVS, Death by Scroll, Unbelievably Crap). 16–25MB pages and "click to enter" gates. This direction works against contact.

## Observed details worth taking
- Death by Scroll has an accessibility toggle in its header.
- Lama Lama's contact block: "Brief us something.", the phone number, and two plain buttons.
- House of Giants puts the price and the CTA in the first phone screen, with no gate.

## Rejected
- Directions 4 and 5, because they hide or gate content.
- Editorial-minimal (Signal-A, Burgundy), because it is our own house style.
