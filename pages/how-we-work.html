How we work

Most studios say they'll discover, design, build and launch. We do too. Here's what working with us is like day to day.

You deal with one person

The person who answers your email is the person building your site. You'll get a reply within two working days, from them, every time.

A short note after every working day

What changed, what was tested, what hasn't been tested yet, and why we chose one option over another. You'll never have to ask where things are.

Tested on a small phone before it's finished

We check every page on a small phone screen and show you what we found. On FarmCompanion, one of our own sites, the main button started below the first screen. We moved it up so visitors see it without scrolling.
