Farm Companion

A map of UK farm shops and what's in season. Most people arrive on a phone, looking for one shop by name.

Who for
People in the UK looking for a farm shop near them
Built with
Next.js, Postgres, Meilisearch, MapLibre
Live at
farmcompanion.co.uk

How people use it

Almost nobody browses the directory. They search for one farm shop by name and land on its page.

Google Search Console showed it plainly: nearly every search visit landed on a shop page, and the guide pages brought in close to none. So we dropped a planned navigation redesign and treated the farm page as the product.

Smaller calls

The first button, on the first screen

On a small phone, a tall picture at the top pushed the page's first button below the first screen. We cut the picture down so "Show on map" sits halfway down the first screen, and made every control at least big enough to tap.

Say what we don't know

No shop's opening hours were stored in a form the site could read, so the hours box sat empty on every page. Each page now says "Opening hours not confirmed" instead of leaving a blank.

A test has to fail first

Map search crashed whenever a shop had no county on record. The first test for the fix passed against the broken code, so it proved nothing. We rewrote it until it failed on the bug, then fixed the bug.
