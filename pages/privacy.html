Privacy notice

Last updated: 21 November 2026.

Who we are

eazy/access is the trading name of EAZY ACCESS LTD, a company registered in England and Wales, company number 13205428. Registered office: 71–75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London WC2H 9JQ. We're registered with the Information Commissioner's Office, reference ZC137268.

We decide how your information is used, which makes us the data controller. For anything about your information, email info@eazyaccess.org.

What we collect

  • From the contact form: your name, email address, what you need and your message.
  • If you email us: whatever your email contains.
  • If you message us on WhatsApp: your phone number, your WhatsApp name and photo, and whatever you send.
  • When you visit: your IP address and browser details reach our hosting provider, Cloudflare, as they would with any website. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which records the page, how fast it loaded and the type of device, not who you are. It sets no cookies. We don't run advertising.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry and, if you'd like to go ahead, to agree the work with you. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before a contract (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).

We also use your IP address and the spam check on the form to stop automated abuse. That's our legitimate interest in keeping the form working (Article 6(1)(f)).

We count visits to see which pages people read and whether they load quickly, so we can improve the site. That's our legitimate interest too, and the count doesn't identify you.

We never sell your information, and we don't use it for marketing.

On WhatsApp we only reply to messages you send us. Tell us to stop, or block us, and we won't message you again.

Who handles it for us

  • Cloudflare hosts this site, runs the spam check on the form and counts visits.
  • Resend delivers your message to our inbox and sends you a confirmation email.
  • Google Workspace runs our email, which is where your message is kept.
  • WhatsApp (WhatsApp LLC, part of Meta) carries messages you send us there. They're end-to-end encrypted: WhatsApp can see your number and when you messaged us, but not what you said. It handles your contact details for us under its data processing terms, and anything else it holds about you under its own privacy policy.
  • Apple stores an encrypted backup of our WhatsApp chats in iCloud. Only we hold the key, so Apple can't read it.
  • Adobe serves the fonts on this site, so it receives your IP address and counts page views to work out the font licence. It handles this under its own privacy policy.

Some of these companies are based in the United States, so your information may be processed there. Cloudflare, Resend, Google and WhatsApp work for us under data processing agreements with the safeguards UK law requires for transfers abroad. Adobe handles font requests under its own privacy policy. Our iCloud backup may be stored outside the UK, but it's encrypted so only we can read it.

How long we keep it

This website doesn't store your message. It passes it straight to our inbox. Resend, which delivers it, keeps a copy in its logs for a limited time, and Cloudflare's logs keep request details, including your IP address, for a few days. We keep enquiry emails and WhatsApp chats for two years after we last heard from you, then delete them. Our next WhatsApp backup then replaces the old one. WhatsApp keeps its own records under its own policy. If your enquiry turns into a project, we keep what's needed for that project and our accounts.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, object to how we use it, ask us to limit its use, or ask for it in a form you can take elsewhere. Email info@eazyaccess.org and we'll reply within one month, as the law requires.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
