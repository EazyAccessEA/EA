Terms of business

Last updated: 25 November 2026.

These terms apply when we design or build something for you. Each project also has a written proposal. The proposal and these terms together are our agreement, and if they ever disagree, the proposal wins.

Who we are

eazy/access is the trading name of EAZY ACCESS LTD, a company registered in England and Wales, company number 13205428. Registered office: 71–75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London WC2H 9JQ. Email: info@eazyaccess.org.

Some parts of these terms apply only to consumers, meaning people buying for themselves rather than for a business, charity or club. Those parts say so.

The proposal

Before any work starts, we send you a proposal. It sets out what we'll make, the stages, a fixed price for each stage, and the launch date. The agreement starts when you accept the proposal in writing, and email counts.

The person who replies to your enquiry is the person who does the work. We don't pass your project to anyone else.

Price and payment

  • Each stage has a fixed price. We won't charge more for a stage unless you agree a change in writing first.
  • We invoice each stage before it starts, and the invoice is due within 14 days. The stage starts once it's paid.
  • We're not registered for VAT, so no VAT is added to our prices. If that changes, we'll tell you before we price any new stage.
  • Domains, hosting and other services are bought in your name and paid by you directly to the provider. We'll tell you what each one costs before you sign up.

Changes

If you want something that isn't in the proposal, we'll tell you in writing what it costs and how it moves the launch date. Nothing changes until you agree.

What we need from you

We'll need your text, images, logins and approvals at the points the proposal sets out. If they arrive late, the launch date moves by the same amount and we'll confirm the new date in writing. You must have the right to use anything you give us, such as photos and logos.

The launch date

We agree the launch date before work starts. If we can't meet it for a reason on our side, we'll tell you as soon as we know, with a new date. If you're a consumer and we miss the date by an unreasonable amount, you can end the agreement and get back what you paid for the work we haven't done.

Who owns what

  • Your domain, hosting and logins are in your name from day one.
  • The design and code we make for a stage become yours once that stage is paid for.
  • Fonts, plugins, stock images and other outside services stay under their own licences. We'll tell you about any that cost money or need renewing.
  • We only show your project in our portfolio if you say yes in writing.

After launch

If anything we made doesn't work as the proposal describes, tell us within 30 days of launch and we'll fix it free of charge. After that, updates, help with hosting and other changes are priced separately. Nothing renews automatically, and we never charge for ongoing work you haven't agreed to.

Your right to cancel (consumers)

If you're a consumer, you can cancel within 14 days of accepting the proposal, for any reason. We'll refund what you've paid within 14 days of you telling us.

If you ask us to start work within those 14 days and then cancel, we'll keep a fair amount for the work we've already done and refund the rest.

To cancel, email info@eazyaccess.org or write to our registered office. You can use the form below, but you don't have to.

To EAZY ACCESS LTD, 71–75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London WC2H 9JQ, info@eazyaccess.org:

Ordered on: [date]. Name: [your name]. Address: [your address]. Signature (only if sent on paper). Date: [date].

Ending the agreement

You can end the agreement at any time by telling us in writing. We'll keep payment for the work done in the current stage, refund the rest of that stage, and hand over everything you've paid for.

We can end it if an invoice is still unpaid 14 days after we've reminded you, or if you ask us to do something unlawful. The same refund and handover apply.

Your personal data and your customers'

How we handle your own details is in our privacy notice.

If our work means we handle personal data on your behalf, such as your customers' details in a shop or a form, we act only on your written instructions. We keep it confidential and secure, and we only use other providers to process it if you agree. We help you answer requests from the people it belongs to and report any breach to you without delay. When the work ends, we delete it or give it back, whichever you choose, and give you whatever information you need to check we've kept to this.

Confidentiality

We each keep private anything the other shares in confidence, unless the law requires otherwise.

Liability

We'll do the work with reasonable care and skill. Nothing in these terms limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or anything else the law doesn't allow us to limit.

If you're a consumer, we're responsible for loss you suffer that was a foreseeable result of us breaking this agreement or failing to use reasonable care and skill. Your legal rights, for example under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, aren't affected by these terms.

If you're a business, our total liability for a project is limited to the amount you've paid us for it, and we're not liable for lost profit, lost revenue or other indirect loss.

We're not responsible for outages or changes at hosting companies, domain registrars or other outside services.

Complaints

If you're unhappy, email info@eazyaccess.org. We'll reply and try to put it right. If you're a consumer and we can't settle it between us, Citizens Advice can tell you what your options are.

Changes to these terms

The terms that apply to your project are the ones in place when you accepted the proposal. If we update these terms later, that doesn't change an agreement you've already made.

Law

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales. If you're a consumer living in Scotland or Northern Ireland, you can also bring a claim in the courts where you live.
