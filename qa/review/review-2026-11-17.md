## Verdict

**Not ready to publish.** Nine blockers, all fixable in one sitting, one of which needs your decision (B8). The brand system holds across every page;
the failures are in-between states, mid-size screens and contrast, not the design itself.

## Blockers — fix before publishing

**B1. No 404 page.** A missing URL returns `404` with an empty body: a blank white screen, no header, no way back.
`curl -i localhost:8788/nope` → `Content-Length: 0`; `state-404.png`. *Artisan, confirmed.*
Fix: add `pages/404.html` (header, "Page not *found*", links to Work and Contact, footer) and set
`assets.not_found_handling: "404-page"` in `wrangler.jsonc`.

**B2. The work fan breaks between 821 and ~1200px.** At 1024 the open sheet's image covers its own name, line and
button, and closed sheets clip their names. `1024-home.png`. *Artisan, confirmed.*
Fix: use the phone swipe row below 1200px, or stack the open sheet's image under its text in that range.

**B3. Text below 4.5:1 on brand colours.** *axe, measured.*
- Tool cards (home): "Run / 1 of 4" and "Used on" use 85% of the text colour and fail on Adobe red (3.53),
  Hetzner red (4.14), Coolify purple (3.80); the chips' tinted fill fails on Coolify (3.81) and Redis (4.38).
  Fix: full text colour for the labels; chips with no fill and a 1px outline.
- /work: sheet numbers in orange on ground, 3.2; deeper's pink labels on red, 3.84 (`.kind`) and 4.30 (`.sheet-lede`);
  "Our *work*" on the list sheet at 375, 2.95. Flintmere's page: "Next project" label on deeper red, 3.84.
  Fix: `--orange-text` for small orange text; white for deeper's secondary text.
- Decorative giant tool names also trip axe (1.1–1.5). They are hidden from screen readers and exempt as
  decoration, but render them from a `data-` attribute via `::after` so the audit stays clean.

**B4. "Sent" is invisible on a phone.** After sending, the confirmation appears below the form, off screen; the
visible screen does not change. `state-form-sent.png`. *Artisan, confirmed.*
Fix: replace the form card's contents with the confirmation, scroll it into view and move focus to it.

**B5. /work list sheet cuts off "06 Call and Consequence" at 375.** `375-work.png`. *Artisan, confirmed.*
Fix: let the list sheet grow to its content (or add 24px bottom padding).

**B6. The sticky "Let's talk" bar covers content on phones.** It sits over the tools deck's "1 of 26" counter
(`state-deck-jump.png`) and over the big wordmark at 768 (`768-home.png`). *Artisan, confirmed on the deck.*
Fix: hide the bar while the tools deck is on screen (as it already does for the "Let's talk" band), and pad the
hero's foot by the bar's height.

**B7. A form error loses keyboard focus.** Send disables the button while sending, which drops focus to the
page body; nothing moves it to the first error. `main.js` line 192. *Advocate, confirmed in code.* (WCAG 2.4.3, 3.3.1)
Fix: `aria-disabled` plus a sending flag instead of `disabled`; on error focus the first invalid field, on success the status.

**B8. How we work makes a promise you haven't confirmed.** "A short note after every working day … You'll never
have to ask where things are." It is not on the five promises in CLAUDE.md, and the home page no longer shows
that note. *Advocate, confirmed in `pages/how-we-work.html`.* Decide: confirm it, or cut it.

**B9. The hero lines sway forever with no pause.** `.hero-lines path{animation:sway 7s … infinite}` — moving content
beside text for more than 5 s needs a way to stop it (WCAG 2.2.2, Level A); reduced motion alone doesn't satisfy it.
*Advocate, confirmed in `styles.css` line 84.* Fix: let the sway run one cycle and stop, or add a small pause control.

## Should fix

**S0. Links that say one thing and do another** (*Advocate, confirmed in markup*):
- The home fan's "Read the project" goes to a `/work/#sheet`, not the project page. Point it at `/work/<name>`.
- /work list rows have real `href`s to the project pages but a click opens a sheet instead (middle-click navigates).
  Let rows navigate; keep the sheet behaviour on the sheet edges.
- /work's six "Read the project" links all announce the same name. Name the project in each.

**S0b. Keyboard and screen reader** (*Advocate*):
- /work takes over the arrow keys for the whole page (`document` keydown with `preventDefault`), breaking scrolling
  and screen-reader reading. Listen only when focus is inside the sheets, and announce "Sheet 3 of 7: Flintmere".
- The desktop tools section is 26 tab stops before "Start a project". Make the tiles one stop (arrow keys between them).
- The phone menu has no `role="dialog"`/`aria-modal`, and the header and footer stay reachable behind it.
- At 375 the contact page shows the form first but tabs to the intro, email and phone first (CSS `order`).
  Make the markup order match what's shown.
- The phone tools deck has no previous/next buttons for people who can't swipe.

**S0c. Sticky bar on the legal pages** (*Advocate*): with no "Let's talk" band to hide it, the bar sits over the
company details at the foot of /privacy, /cookies and /terms, and shows beside the header's "Let's talk". Hide it there.

**S0d. Copying the email gives "infoeazyaccess.org"** (*Advocate, confirmed in `styles.css` line 328*): the "@" is
CSS-only. Since `main.js` already rebuilds the link, have it insert a real "@".

**S1. The intro line comes before the headline.** On every width the first thing read is "eazy/access is a small
UK studio…", above "Websites people *actually use.*" *Your question; I agree.*
Fix: headline first, that sentence directly under it as the subline. It stays on the home page (it is what
tells a stranger what the studio is); there is no About page, How we work does that job.

**S2. Performance 93–94.** *Lighthouse, measured.*
- Adobe Fonts serves no `font-display`, so text waits for the fonts. Set *font-display: swap* in the Adobe web project.
- Images are sent far larger than shown: fan details are 640×800 for 118–300px slots (Call and Consequence wastes
  112 KB); /work phone shots likewise. Fix: a 320px version plus `srcset`/`sizes`.

**S3. Tool colours put a second orange and a neon on the page.** Cloudflare `#F48120` sits near the slash orange
and the orange "Let's talk" band; Vitest `#77FF00` is fluorescent (`state-deck-jump.png`). *Artisan; a design call.*
You chose full brand colour; the alternative is an ink card with a brand-colour top strip for colours within ~20°
of our orange, and the darker logo variant for fluorescents.

**S4. Phone tool cards are 530px tall against 430px in Paper.** Every card stretches to the busiest one
(Cloudflare Workers, five sites), leaving short cards half empty. *Paper comparison.*
Fix: cap the chip list at three plus "+2 more" on phones, or let the deck's cards size to their own content.

**S5. "FarmCompanion" is spelled two ways.** "Farm Companion" on the home fan, "FarmCompanion" on /work and its
page. The live site's logotype is two words. *Artisan, confirmed in the markup.* Fix: one spelling everywhere.

**S6. /work sheet edge names break mid-word.** "Conse-quence", "deeper. tools". `1440-work.png`. *Artisan.*
Fix: `hyphens:none`, break only between words.

**S7. Side margins differ between sections.** Footer, "Let's talk" band and /work sheet sit at different insets
from the text (16, 8 and 0px against 20px at 375; 16 against 40px at 1440). *Artisan, not measured by me.*
Fix: one gutter for every panel.

**S8. Header has no backing on phones.** Section content shows through behind the wordmark when scrolled.
*Artisan, visible in `state-deck-jump.png`.* Fix: ground colour at ~92% behind the header; `scroll-margin-top` on headings.

**S9. Tablet (768) hero is mostly empty hairlines** and pushes the wordmark under the sticky bar. `768-home.png`.
*Artisan.* Fix: use the phone hero layout up to 1023px.

**S10. Smaller inconsistencies** (*Artisan*): the facts table sits at different heights on different project pages
(align it to the breadcrumb); the "Build" pill on How we work is twice the width of the others (equal columns);
the tool card grows mid-opening then settles (fix its height).

**S11. Structure** (*axe, moderate*): an `h3` follows the `h1` with no `h2` on How we work and Contact; the sticky bar
sits outside any landmark. Fix: promote those headings; put the bar inside `<main>` or an `aside`.

## Polish

- **P1.** The big wordmark's slash draws in last (~2.2 s), so a fast scroller sees "eazy access". Fill the slash first.
- **P2.** The FarmCompanion showcase shadow is near-black on a pale panel; tint shadows from the panel colour.
- **P3.** At 1920 the big wordmark centres while the headline keeps the 40px gutter; cap the hero at 1440 or left-align.
- **P5.** "Selected work / Open all six" implies more than six exist; "Our work / See all six" says what's there. (*Advocate*)
- **P6.** The daily note is labelled "Note 2 of 8" to screen readers though only one shows; use "Today's note". (*Advocate*)
- **P7.** Contact's Send button sits at 942–998px on a 812px phone, 1.2 screens down: at the limit. (*Advocate, measured*)
- **P4.** Numbers are styled four ways (orange small, huge italic, 20px ink); pick ink/secondary italic everywhere
  and keep orange for the accent word and the slash.

## Matches the approved designs

Fan with opening sheet, branded tool cards and their opening, project showcase (browser + phone), reflection note,
phone hero and phone tools deck all match their Paper artboards on page "v5 — pages", apart from S4 and B6.

## Keep

- The slash system: hairlines on the slash angle into the outlined wordmark, one orange IvyPresto word per heading.
- Each project on a panel in its own brand colour, from the home fan to the "Next project" band.
- The quiet pages: legal, How we work and the contact form keep a tight measure and one dark button per screen.
- A 2px orange focus ring on every control; every non-inline tap target ≥ 24px (most 40–44); reduced motion
  turns every animation off; the form has labels, a fieldset, a live status and a no-JS fallback. (*Advocate*)
