# Opening lines: 20 written, 3 kept

Sources: voice-of-customer.md (86 complaints) and pricing-research.md. The theme rank is in brackets.

## Being ghosted (#2, 19–25 complaints)
1. Paid a web designer, then heard nothing?
2. Your web designer has stopped replying.
3. Message the person building your site. On WhatsApp.
4. No account manager and no ticket number. The person who replies builds your site.
5. Still waiting for your web designer to call you back?

## Hidden fees (#1, 20 complaints)
6. A "free" website isn't free.
7. £149 a month for two years is £3,576, and the site still isn't yours.
8. The price you're quoted is the price you pay.
9. A fixed price for each stage. Nothing extra unless you say yes in writing.
10. Know the whole cost before you pay a penny.

## Never delivered (#3, 14 complaints)
11. Six months on and still no website?
12. Your launch date, in writing, before work starts.
13. Know the day your site goes live before you pay.

## Poor quality (#4, 14 complaints)
14. Not a template. Built by hand for your business.
15. A site that sounds like your business, not like everyone else's.

## Domain held (#6, 5–7 complaints, rare but severe)
16. Who owns your website address? Make sure it's you.
17. Your domain, your hosting, your logins, from day one.
18. Leave whenever you like. It's all in your name already.

## Combined
19. One person, one price per stage, one WhatsApp chat.
20. Before you pay a web designer, ask them three questions.

## The three kept

**A. Ghosted (#1)**
- Image: a WhatsApp chat with a real-looking reply from the person building the site. Draft messages only, no screenshot of a real customer.
- Why: it answers the second-biggest complaint, and the ad's own button proves the point. No UK web-design ad found uses click-to-WhatsApp.

**B. The free-site catch (#7 + #9)**
- Image: the sum. £149 × 24 months = £3,576 against one fixed price that's yours, plus "a fixed price for each stage".
- Why: it answers the biggest complaint. It needs a real price from you.

**C. Three questions (#20)**
- Image: a short list. How fast do you reply? Is this the whole price? Whose name is the domain in? Each one is answered by us.
- Why: it covers themes 1, 2, 3 and 6 in one image, and it reads like advice rather than an ad. A slight risk that it's too wordy at feed size.
