# Bradford agreement: Shopify ownership section (draft, 26 Nov 2026)

For the agreement with the Bradford client taking over the store now called Athari Press (cvazpv-pu.myshopify.com). Drop it into the "Who owns what" section when the agreement is built. Square brackets are details still to confirm with the owner.

## Clause

> The shop is an existing Shopify store, which we'll rename [new name]. On [date], we transfer ownership of the store to you, using an email address you control. From then on the store, its Shopify plan and billing, and every login are yours.
>
> We keep collaborator access, with only the permissions you approve, while this agreement says we're working on the store. You can remove that access at any time in Shopify (Settings, Users).
>
> The domain [domain] moves into your name on the same date.
>
> Shopify pays us nothing for this store, because it was already open before you took it over.

## Before it goes in

- Client's legal name and the email address the store transfers to.
- The new store name and its domain, and who holds that domain today.
- The transfer date, and whether it comes before or after launch.
- Scope, price per stage and launch date for the rest of the agreement (CLAUDE.md promises: fixed price per stage, launch date agreed first; no VAT added).
- Whether this store will ever be rebuilt as a new development store. If so, the transfer-store clause in `plan.md` replaces the last paragraph.

## Why this wording

- Shopify pays commission only on a store created as a development store and transferred, or one opened through an approved affiliate link (Partner Program Agreement Part B s.1.1 to 1.3). This store was neither, so s.2.4 disclosure is met by saying nothing is paid.
- Agreement Part B s.6: the studio must have a written agreement with the client for its work on the store.
- The promise in CLAUDE.md: the client owns the domain, hosting and logins; this clause says when.
