# Gmail reply templates

Save each one in Gmail: Settings, See all settings, Advanced, Templates: Enable, Save. Then write a new email,
paste the text, and choose the three dots, Templates, Save draft as template. To use one, open the reply,
choose the three dots, Templates, and pick it.

Replace anything in [square brackets] before sending. Each promise here is one the owner has confirmed
(CLAUDE.md, 16 Nov 2026). Don't add others.
