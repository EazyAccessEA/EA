# Quick question: which of these would make you get in touch?

Thanks for helping. This takes about two minutes, and there are no wrong answers.

Picture this: you run a small business and you need a new website. You're looking at small web studios. Below are three ways one studio could describe itself. Read all three, then answer the questions underneath.

**A.** One person builds your site, and that same person answers your emails, within two working days.

**B.** After every working day on your site, you get a short note: what changed, what was tested, and what hasn't been tested yet.

**C.** Your domain, hosting and logins are in your name from day one, and each stage has a fixed price agreed in writing before we start.

## Your answers

1. Which one makes you most likely to get in touch? (A, B or C)
2. Why that one? Please answer in your own words, one or two sentences.
3. In your own words, how is this studio different from others you've seen?
4. Is there anything in any of them you don't believe, or that worries you?

Thank you. Your answers are only used to improve this page, and your name won't appear anywhere.
