Why move

What changes when the shop runs on Shopify instead of your own setup.

Shopify charges a monthly plan for this. We tell you which plan fits before you decide.

Your shop now

  • Hosting, updates and security are partly your job
  • Checkout and payments pieced together from plugins
  • Selling on Google or Instagram means another tool

On Shopify

  • Shopify runs the hosting, updates and security
  • Shopify's own checkout, with Shop Pay built in
  • Google and Instagram sales from the same shop

What moves

Your products, photos and prices come across. Your old web addresses point to the new pages, so links people already use keep working.

Before we start, we list everything your current shop holds and agree with you what moves and what stays behind. Customer accounts and past orders depend on what your current platform lets us export, so we check that first and tell you what we found.

In writing

You own the shop

We build your shop in a Shopify development store and hand it to you before launch. From then on the shop, its plan and every login are yours. While you stay on a paid plan, Shopify pays us a referral commission of up to 20% of your monthly subscription. Your price is the same either way, and your agreement says so.

A fixed price per stage

Nothing extra gets added unless you agree to it in writing first. The launch date is set before any work begins.
