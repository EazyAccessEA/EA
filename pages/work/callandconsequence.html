Call and Consequence

Illustrated films about business decisions: what was decided, why it made sense at the time, and what followed. Every figure links to the filing or interview it came from.

Who for
Anyone curious about why companies decide what they do
Built with
Cloudflare Workers, React
Live at

What it does

Each film comes with its chapters, its sources and a full transcript.

Point at any figure on a film's page and a card shows where it came from. The films so far cover JCPenney, Webvan and MoviePass.

Smaller calls

A figure without a source doesn't build

The build stops if any figure on screen has no matching entry in the list of sources.

Numbers are checked as they're spoken

The narration once misread a dollar figure, putting the word "dollars" in twice, and the voice check let it through. The film was re-narrated, and fixes now go into the spoken text itself.

Nothing from other companies

The site sets no cookies and loads no trackers. The only outside content is the YouTube player, on its privacy-enhanced domain. The films say plainly that the voice is synthetic.
